\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Counter-guided target evidence search uses paired orbits during training while retaining target-only Student deployment. Geometry-free training and post-hoc evaluation separate mapping gains from pixel exclusion. Current inference controls support sensitivity to correct complementary guidance and reject arbitrary random sampling. However, center sampling remains competitive, and overlapping samples favor an interpretation involving scale/weight allocation rather than large spatial displacement. The principal Student improves regional mapping, while completed component controls delimit the contribution of each objective without establishing multi-seed superiority.
