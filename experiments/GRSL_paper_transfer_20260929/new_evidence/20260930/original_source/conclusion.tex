\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented counter-guided target evidence search for single-orbit SAR landslide mapping. The complementary orbit guides the Teacher search policy during training; the deployed Student uses only target-orbit information. Training uses all labelled pixels, and geometry masks are applied only when evaluating the resulting predictions. The Student improves over its target-only baseline in both the target-valid region and the full region, with an additional gain in the target-distorted diagnostic. The counter-guidance comparison, search intervention, and loss-component study support the proposed mechanism; matched component ablations for the selected two-stage Student remain to be completed.
