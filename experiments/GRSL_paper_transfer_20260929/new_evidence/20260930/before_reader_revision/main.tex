\begin{abstract}
Rapid landslide mapping with synthetic aperture radar (SAR) must contend with view-dependent backscatter and side-looking geometric distortion. Paired ascending and descending observations can be used during training, although only one orbit may be available for rapid inference. We propose a counter-guided Teacher that uses the complementary orbit to guide a search policy while retrieving evidence only from the target orbit. A target-only Student distills that policy and the retrieved target evidence. We train on all labelled pixels and evaluate the same predictions both with and without excluding geometrically distorted target pixels. On the target-valid region of the Haiti paired-orbit benchmark, the Student improves AUPRC from 87.55\% to 88.77\% and IoU from 62.27\% to 64.65\% over its target-only baseline. On the full region, AUPRC rises from 87.98\% to 89.16\% and IoU from 62.91\% to 65.10\%. A separate target-distorted diagnostic shows an IoU gain from 64.14\% to 65.64\%. These results show improved single-orbit mapping across valid and distorted evaluation regions. Current inference interventions show sensitivity to counter pairing and random sampling, while fixed-center sampling remains competitive.
\end{abstract}
