documents under `docs/`. The compact paper evidence is under
`experiments/GRSL_complete_paper_package/`.

- `final_haiti_dataset_audit.md`

- `external_baselines_and_kd_cards.md`

experiment plans were moved unchanged to `archive/history/reports/`. Their
recorded values remain available for audit but do not define the final method.
